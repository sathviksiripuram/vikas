Students rarely lose an admission because of their grades. They lose it because a transcript arrived late, a recommender went quiet, or a financial document was in the wrong format.

Here is the complete checklist, in the order you should tackle it.

## Start three months out

### Academic records
- All mark sheets and transcripts, from Class 10 onwards
- Provisional or degree certificate
- Consolidated marks memo
- Backlog certificate, where your university issues one

Order extra attested copies now. Universities often want originals or certified copies, and your college will take longer than you expect.

### Passport
- Valid for at least six months beyond your intended travel date
- Check that the spelling exactly matches your academic records

A name mismatch between passport and transcripts is one of the most common causes of delay. Fix it before you apply, not after.

## Start two months out

### Statement of Purpose (SOP)
Your SOP explains why this course, why this university, and why you. It is the one document where you control the narrative. Expect three or four drafts before it is ready.

### Letters of Recommendation (LOR)
- Usually two academic, sometimes one professional
- Approach recommenders early and give them your CV and SOP
- Confirm they will submit before the deadline

### Resume / CV
One page, academic-focused. Include projects, internships, publications and any relevant work experience.

## Start one month out

### English proficiency score
IELTS, TOEFL or PTE — booked, taken, and reported directly to your universities. Build in time for a retake.

### Aptitude test score
GRE or GMAT, where the programme requires it.

## For the visa stage

- Admission or offer letter
- Proof of financial resources — bank statements, loan sanction letter, scholarship letter or a blocked account, depending on the country
- Health insurance
- Medical examination reports, where required
- Accommodation details, for some destinations

> Financial documentation rules differ sharply by country. Germany, for example, commonly involves a blocked account; other destinations accept bank statements or a loan sanction letter. Always confirm the current requirement for your specific destination.

## The mistake that costs students most

Waiting for offers before starting visa paperwork. Financial documents, medicals and police clearances all take time, and the visa queue does not care that your offer arrived late.

Run your application and your visa preparation in parallel.
